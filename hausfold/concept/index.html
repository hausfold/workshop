hausfold

We rebuild the Mac.

hausfold makes the software that turns a Mac into a workspace someone actually designed. The whole desktop — tiling, launcher, bar, shell, terminal, colours — declared once in a single file and themed end to end, plus the small native tools that live inside it.

None of it is a skin. It is the machine, rebuilt: reproducible on any Mac you sit down at, and reversible the moment you change your mind. Take it as it comes, or take it apart.

The index

Six pieces
  • nebelhaus The workspace. A whole macOS desktop in one declarative file. Price and licence: Free · MIT
  • pounce The launcher. Native, instant, and teachable — you write the commands. Price and licence: Free · MIT
  • perch The shelf. Files park in the notch on their way somewhere else. Free up to three. Price and licence: $19 · once
  • nebelung The palette. A quieter set of colours, one theme, every tool. Price and licence: Free · MIT
  • holt The substrate. Parallel coding agents, isolated, in any repo. Price and licence: Free · MIT
  • revena The builder. Answer a few questions, walk away with your machine. Status: In the workshop

Everything free is MIT and stays that way. perch is $19 once, which includes a year of updates; keeping them after that is $9 a year, and the copy you bought keeps working either way.
