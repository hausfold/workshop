hausfold

We rebuild the Mac.

hausfold makes the software that turns a Mac into a workspace someone actually designed. The whole desktop — tiling, launcher, bar, shell, terminal, colours — declared once in a single file and themed end to end, plus the small native tools that live inside it.

None of it is a skin. It is the machine, rebuilt: reproducible on any Mac you sit down at, and reversible the moment you change your mind. Take it as it comes, or take it apart.

The index

Six pieces
  • nebelhaus — the whole desktop, declared in one file.
  • pounce — a launcher you teach your own commands.
  • perch — files park in the notch on their way somewhere else.
  • nebelung — a quieter set of colours, shared by everything.
  • holt — parallel coding agents that never collide.
  • revena — a rice builder. Still in the workshop.
